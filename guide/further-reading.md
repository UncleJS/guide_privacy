## General guides

- [Electronic Frontier Foundation: Surveillance Self-Defense](https://ssd.eff.org). Guides on threat modeling, messaging, devices, and specific situations such as protests and border crossings.
- [Privacy Guides](https://www.privacyguides.org). Community-maintained recommendations for tools and services, with criteria for each category.
- [Mozilla: Privacy Not Included](https://foundation.mozilla.org/en/privacynotincluded/). Reviews of the privacy practices of connected products.

## Accounts and breaches

- [Have I Been Pwned](https://haveibeenpwned.com). Check whether an email address appears in known breaches, and subscribe to alerts.
- [FIDO Alliance: Passkeys](https://fidoalliance.org). Background on passkeys and hardware security keys.

## Messaging and tools

- [Signal Support](https://support.signal.org). Setup, usernames, disappearing messages, and safety numbers.
- [Tor Project](https://www.torproject.org). Download and verification, bridges, and usage guidance.

[↑ Back to TOC](#toc)

## Platform security documentation

- [Apple Platform Security](https://support.apple.com/guide/security/welcome/web). How Apple devices protect data, including backups and Advanced Data Protection.
- [Google Android security](https://source.android.com/docs/security) and the [Google Account help center](https://support.google.com/accounts). Device and account security settings.
- [Microsoft Windows security documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/windows/security/). Device encryption, BitLocker, and privacy controls.

[↑ Back to TOC](#toc)

## Help for higher-risk situations

- [Access Now Digital Security Helpline](https://www.accessnow.org/help/). Free, confidential help for civil-society groups, journalists, and activists.
- [Coalition Against Stalkerware](https://stopstalkerware.org). Resources for people who suspect stalkerware, and for supporters.
- [Safety Net, National Network to End Domestic Violence](https://www.techsafety.org). Technology safety planning for survivors and advocates.
- [Committee to Protect Journalists](https://cpj.org). Safety resources for journalists.

If you live outside the United States, look for the equivalent national or regional organizations for digital rights, domestic-abuse support, and legal aid.

[↑ Back to TOC](#toc)

## Laws and rights

- [EU GDPR overview](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection_en) and your national data-protection authority, for access, deletion, and objection rights.
- Your state or national consumer-privacy law (for example California's CCPA/CPRA), for opt-out and deletion requests.
- A lawyer or digital-rights organization in your country, for border searches, device seizure, and evidence questions.
